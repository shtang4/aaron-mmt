R is net P&L divided by planned risk (margin × |entry − stop| ÷ entry × leverage). Expectancy is the mean net P&L per trade. Profit factor is gross wins ÷ gross losses. Breakeven win rate is 1 ÷ (1 + avg win ÷ avg loss). Drawdown is the largest peak-to-trough fall of the equity curve. A "clean" trade has no rule broken.
