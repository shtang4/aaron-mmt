# 08 — Session transcript, extracted

Running log of what the spoken session adds to, or changes in, the slide notes. Partial transcript, supplied in pieces; each part is filed against the module it covers. Spoken words are treated as authoritative over the slides where they differ, on the same basis that the session settled the handouts as supplementary.

## Part 1: opening, Module 0 and Module 1

### Mindset (Module 0)

- **"The difference is not the signal, the difference is execution."** Same coin, same direction, same call; some students profit and some lose, because they enter, manage and exit differently. "You don't need more signal."
- Stated aim: "to become your own fisherman." After the two days the student should not need calls. Dr. Aaron continues to share coins but frames them as practice material, not the product.
- The pyramid restated in his words: knowledge → must be **enlightened** into wisdom → wisdom is useless until **executed** → execution becomes success.
- Trading challenges and competitions are mentioned as a way "to train your trading mindset and power your strategy." The 1,993 → 18,288 USDT card in Module 5 may be from such a challenge; not stated.
- "Hybrid trader, you can combine all": the explicit goal is a student who can mix scalping with other styles after the course. That is the license under which the two-week ZETA hold and the one-day AERGO hold sit inside a "scalping" course.

### Scalping definition (Module 1, section 1)

- **Holding period, in his words: "by minutes, hours, not more than days. Less than 24 hours."** And: "some go to seconds, but I don't think you can trade secondly." That replaces the slide's textbook "seconds to minutes" with the taught rule: **minutes to hours, never past 24 hours.** It is the concrete form of Golden Rule 6 ("do not hold, we are not doing swing trades"). By this rule the ZETA (two weeks), BMT (five days) and AERGO (over 24 hours) showcase trades were not scalps.
- Prerequisites as spoken: handle stress, make rapid decisions, monitor several things at once but execute on a very short timeframe.
- Live trading during the session was "if there is a possibility, depending on the situation." Not promised.

### The 3D method (Module 1, section 3)

- Diagnose, Dose, Discharge stated as on the slides. "The signal is the symptom."
- **The EMA pair on the Diagnose slide is a placeholder.** His words: "Let's say I put short-term EMA is EMA 25 and the long-term EMA is EMA 50 ... for concept explanation. It doesn't matter, later I'm going to teach you the exact one. You just need to understand the concept." So the golden cross and death cross in Module 1, and the stop "probably just below EMA 50", are illustrations. The exact lines are the Module 3 set (9, 25, 55, 155, 255), and the direction rule is EMA 155 on Heikin-Ashi. This closes the EMA 50 question for good: it was never a rule.
- Discharge as spoken: TP at the nearest resistance, "set it first"; exit when the short EMA crosses back below the long EMA, "no question"; stop "probably" just below the long EMA, "structural, not emotional." The word "probably" confirms the stop location on this slide was conceptual; the operative stop is the Module 5 ROI tier beyond the structural level.
- Dose as spoken matches the slide: position size and stop loss, decided before entry, "not just about capital."
