# 00 — Mindset and Course Outline

Source: opening slides of Momentum Mastery Trading (MMT), Dr. Aaron MMT, 2026.

## 1. Mindset: the Know → Enlighten → Do pyramid

The course opens with a three-tier pyramid. Each tier is a level of mastery, and each maps to an outcome:

| Tier | Chinese | Outcome | Chinese |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| Know (base) | 知道 | Knowledge | 知识 |
| Enlighten (middle) | 悟到 | Wisdom | 智慧 |
| Do (top) | 做到 | Success | 成功 |

Reading of the slide:

- **Know** is the widest tier. Absorbing the material gives knowledge, and most students stop here.
- **Enlighten** is understanding *why* the rules exist, so you can apply them in conditions the slides never showed.
- **Do** is the narrowest tier. Only consistent execution produces success. Knowing the method is not the same as trading it.

The pyramid is the frame for the whole course: every module below is meant to be executed, not just learned.

## 2. Stated learning outcomes ("You Will Learn How To")

1. **Scan the market** — see where the market is moving.
2. **Select which coin to trade** — avoid random or blind entries.
3. **Enter the market with the right direction** — know exactly when to Long, Short, or Skip.
4. **EMA sniper entry** — enter at the *right* moment, not the *first* moment.
5. **Risk management + hedging** — protect the account when a trade goes against you.
6. **Live trade and review** — improve execution on the next trades.

Note the deliberate framing in outcome 3: **Skip** is a first-class decision, on equal footing with Long and Short.

## 4. Structure of the method (as implied by the outline)

The six modules form a single pipeline, and each step gates the next:

1. **Filter** the universe of coins to the ones with a trend (Module 2).
2. **Decide direction** on the chosen coin: Long, Short, or Skip (Module 3).
3. **Time the entry and exit** precisely once direction is set (Module 4).
4. **Size and protect** the position, hedging if it turns (Module 5).
5. **Review** the executed trade to improve the next one (Module 6).

Module 1 supplies the definitions and the "3D framework" the rest of the pipeline hangs on.

Each of Modules 2, 3 and 5 pairs a **Basic tool** with an **Advance tool**. The advance tools (Coin Trend Screener, New Indicators, MMT Hedging) are course-specific; the basic tools are standard technical analysis.
